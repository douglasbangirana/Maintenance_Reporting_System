About MMU FixIt

Faults reported on paper get lost. MMU FixIt gives students and staff one place to log a problem, say how urgent it is, and see that it was recorded.

Every report carries a category, an exact location and a priority so the maintenance team can plan the day: emergencies first, routine repairs batched by building.

What we fix

  • Electrical

    Lights, sockets, projectors, power outages.

  • Plumbing

    Leaks, blocked drains, toilets, water supply.

  • Furniture

    Broken desks, chairs, doors and windows.

  • ICT equipment

    Lab computers, Wi-Fi points, network sockets.

  • Grounds

    Paths, drainage channels, fences, compound lighting.

  • Other

    Anything else that is broken or unsafe.
